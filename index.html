CathView

A teaching simulator for coronary angiography projection angles on iPhone and iPad. Rotate a virtual C-arm with the joystick and see the simulated angiogram update in real time.

冠脉造影投照体位的教学模拟 App：用摇杆转动 C 臂，实时查看对应角度下的模拟造影图像。
